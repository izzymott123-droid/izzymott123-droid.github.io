Background:

I've always gravitated toward the messy middle where technology, design, and user experience collide — the space where a clever piece of engineering only matters if it actually makes someone's life easier. I study Cognitive Science with a Specialization in Computing at UCLA, a combination that turned out to be exactly the right training ground for product work. My coursework drives me to sit in on every user interview and read every support ticket to uncover the problem rather than skip to building the solution. My combination of technical fluency and genuine curiosity about why people do what they do is what's carried me through every role I've held: leading a full discovery project on Illumia's Authorized Payer experience from research through prototype testing, driving a checkout-flow redesign at Givingli that lifted conversion by 63%, building and shipping my own product (BruinLease) end to end, while mentoring 13 junior analysts at TAMID Consulting Group. What I want next is more of the same — problems messy enough to need real research, and the room to see a solution through from the first user conversation to the shipped product.
