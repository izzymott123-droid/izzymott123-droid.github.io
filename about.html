About Me!

I've always gravitated toward the messy middle where technology, design, and user experience collide — the space where a clever piece of engineering only matters if it actually makes someone's life easier. That's the thread running through everything I do: I want to build things that reduce friction for real people, not just check a box on a roadmap. Outside of that, you'll usually find me at a house music show, on a soccer field, or planning the next trip — I like moving fast in most areas of my life, not just at work.

That drive is what pulled me toward product management specifically. I study Cognitive Science with a Specialization in Computing at UCLA, which gave me a front-row seat to both sides of the equation — the technical fluency to understand what's buildable, and a genuine curiosity about why people behave the way they do. Every role I've taken on has sharpened that instinct further: leading a full discovery project on Illumia's Authorized Payer experience, driving a checkout redesign at Givingli that lifted conversion by 63%, and building BruinLease from a blank repo to a live product students actually use. Each one taught me something different about the distance between what users say and what they actually need — and I've tried to carry every lesson into the next thing I build.

I'm genuinely open to anything — new tools, new industries, unfamiliar problems. If there's a way to help, I say yes, and I bring the same level of care to the smallest task as I do to the biggest one. That's less a strategy than just who I am.
